Jannik Reinbold

Hi! I am Jannik, a PhD candidate in macroeconomics at Leipzig University. I am also a member of the interdisciplinary DFG Research Training Group ECO-N. I am interested in macroeconomic growth, climate change, and inequality dynamics. I use these lenses to think about questions such as: Is long-term economic growth possible—or desirable—in a warming world? Who are the winners and losers of climate policies? What frictions prevent the implementation of socially optimal policies? How do we assess welfare?
